[Blink] Fix bottom-controls visual viewport scroll bounds

The maximum visual viewport offset now accounts for the bottom browser
controls, matching the viewport container bounds deltas cc applies in
LayerTreeImpl::UpdateViewportContainerSizes(). Previously, offsets
applied by the compositor while the bottom toolbar was hidden were
clamped on the main thread: visualViewport reported a smaller offset
than what was rendered, and main-thread scrolls could not reach the full
scroll range.

Add BrowserControls::UnreportedBottomSizeAdjustment(), mirroring
UnreportedSizeAdjustment() for the top controls, and include it in the
maximum scroll offset. Only the transient scroll bounds change;
visualViewport.height still does not track bottom controls.
