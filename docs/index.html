[SUIT] Roll omnibox_event.proto for NTP_ACTION_CHIPS

This change rolls omnibox_event.proto from http://cl/966104794
to bring in the NTP_ACTION_CHIPS PageClassification.

It also updates the corresponding Chromium-side histogram mappings:
- Adds NTP_ACTION_CHIPS to OmniboxPageClassification in enums.xml
- Adds NTP_ACTION_CHIPS to OmniboxPageContext in histograms.xml

This enables distinguishing New Tab Page Action Chips from standard NTP
ZPS prefetch requests.

NO_IFTTT=LINT.IfChange tags are stripped during proto export from
Google3, causing IfThisThenAnalyzer to fail incorrectly.
omnibox_event.proto is properly updated in this CL.
